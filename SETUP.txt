FRAME TRY-ON MIRROR — v2 (customer wears your real frame)

LINK: https://adrianpdk1999-byte.github.io/tryon-mirror/

HOW CUSTOMERS USE IT
1. Choose English or Bahasa Malaysia.
2. Put on a frame, type the size printed inside the arm (e.g. 52 □ 18 – 145), tap the frame shape.
3. Look at the screen: the AI reads face shape, face width and PD, then takes a photo (3-2-1).
4. Scores out of 10: Design match, Size fit and Overall, each with a reason.
5. "Try another frame" to compare more frames, or "Send to my WhatsApp".
6. Customer types their number and ticks the consent box. The screen then asks for staff.

STAFF: SENDING TO WHATSAPP (needs internet)
1. Exit Guided Access (iPad: triple-click the side/home button) or app pinning (Android).
2. Tap "1 · Save photo" and save the image to Photos/Gallery.
3. Tap "2 · Open WhatsApp". A chat opens with the customer's number and the summary typed.
4. Attach the saved photo and send. Come back to the app, tap "New customer", turn Guided Access back on.
   (If the customer is already a saved contact, "Share photo + text" sends both in one go.)

DEVICE SET-UP (once)
- iPad: open the link in Safari > Share > Add to Home Screen. Open from the icon, do one scan online.
  Then Settings > Accessibility > Guided Access: on. Open the app, triple-click to start Guided Access.
- Android tablet: open the link in Chrome > ⋮ > Add to Home screen > Install. Do one scan online.
  Settings > Security > App pinning to lock it.
- After the first scan it works offline. Only WhatsApp sending needs internet.

PRIVACY: photos stay on the device and are only sent when the customer asks and ticks consent.
The app clears everything and returns to the start screen when left idle.
ACCURACY: sizes and PD are camera estimates (±3–4 mm). Confirm PD with a PD ruler before ordering lenses.
PRACTICE MODE: add ?mock=round to the link (also oval, square, heart, long) to test without a face.
