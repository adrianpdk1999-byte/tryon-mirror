FRAME TRY-ON MIRROR — SETUP (one time, about 5 minutes)

1. PUT IT ONLINE (free)
   - On a computer, unzip tryon-mirror.zip.
   - Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop and sign up free (Google login is fine).
   - Drag the unzipped "app" folder onto the page. Netlify gives you a link like
     https://something.netlify.app  (you can rename it under Site settings).

2. SET UP THE TABLET (Android, Chrome)
   - Open the link in Chrome while on Wi-Fi.
   - Tap English, allow the camera, and do one scan. This downloads the face AI (about 10 MB).
   - Chrome menu (⋮) > "Add to Home screen" > Install.
   - Open it from the home-screen icon. It runs full-screen.
   - Check: finish a scan > "Done · Show staff". The bottom line should say "● Offline ready".
     After that it works with Wi-Fi off.

3. KIOSK TIPS
   - Settings > Display > Screen timeout: longest. The app also keeps the screen awake while in use.
   - Optional: Settings > Security > "App pinning" to lock the tablet to this app.
   - Mount the tablet at face height, upright (portrait), with good light on the face (not behind).
   - The app goes back to the language screen on its own when nobody is using it.

4. PRACTICE MODE (no face needed)
   Add ?mock=round to the link (also oval, square, heart, long) to see a demo face. Useful for training staff.

5. CHANGING THINGS (edit index.html with any text editor, then drag the folder to Netlify again)
   - Shop name:  BRAND = { name: "Demo Optical" }
   - Frames:     the FRAMES list. Each frame needs shape, lens width A, lens height B, bridge DBL,
                 total width W (mm), colour and rim type.
   - Face-shape thresholds: SHAPE_RULES. The staff screen shows the raw ratios
     (L/W, Jaw, Fhd) so you can fine-tune after scanning a few people.
   After any change, also change VERSION in sw.js (e.g. "tryon-v2") so tablets pick up the update.

PRIVACY: the camera image is processed on the tablet only. Nothing is saved or uploaded.
ACCURACY: mm measurements are estimates (±3–4 mm). Confirm PD with a PD ruler or pupillometer before ordering lenses.
